Every permutation, one swap at a time

Start at the identity. Each edge is one swap of two positions. A permutation sits on the level equal to the fewest swaps needed to reach it, which is n minus its number of cycles. Tap a permutation to see which ones lead to it from the level above and which it leads to below.
